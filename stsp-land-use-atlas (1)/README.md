# STSP Land Use Atlas

An interactive atlas of documented events in the conversion of farmland around the Southern Taiwan Science Park (STSP) and the S-Corridor, 1995–2026. Each record is one sourced event — a planning decision, protest, incident or measured change — tagged by tension: land and planning, water, power and energy, housing and daily life, or ecology and heritage. Records inside a special district plan (特定區) are tagged and can be filtered.

Course atlas, AR5958M A3.

## View it

Open `index.html` in a browser, or visit the GitHub Pages site for this repository.

## Files

- `index.html` — the whole site: page, styles, script, the 45 records and the district boundary data in one file.

## External resources

The page loads two things from the web, so it needs an internet connection:

- Leaflet 1.9.4 from cdnjs (map library)
- Barlow Condensed, Source Sans 3 and IBM Plex Mono from Google Fonts

## Data and method

Compiled with Claude, an AI research assistant, from Taiwanese and English-language news (PTS, CNA, UDN, Commercial Times, Liberty Times and others), releases from the NSTC, NDC and city governments, and Kollar (2025). Allegations and inferred dates are flagged in each record. The records are a finding aid; check the original sources before citing.

District boundaries: taiwan-atlas, from Ministry of the Interior data. Science-park markers are approximate locations, not footprints.
