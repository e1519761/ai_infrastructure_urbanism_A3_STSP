# Cane Fields to Fabs

An interactive map of documented events in the conversion of farmland around the
Southern Taiwan Science Park (STSP) and the southern "S-Corridor", 1995–2026.
Made for AR5958M (AI, Infrastructure, Urbanism), Assignment 3.

**Open the map:** `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`

## What is on the map
- **36 records.** One record = one documented occurrence (a decision, protest,
  incident or measured change) tied to land conversion around an STSP campus.
- **Symbols** show the type of tension: field grid = land & planning,
  droplet = water, bolt = power & energy, house = housing & daily life,
  leaf = ecology & heritage.
- **Precision:** filled symbol = named site; hollow dashed symbol = district
  centroid only; dashed circle = regional event.

## Files
- `index.html` – the whole site (map, filters, timeline, records). Leaflet loads from cdnjs.
- `data/stsp_events.csv` – the records as a spreadsheet, with coordinates and sources.
- `data/stsp_events.json` – the same records as JSON.

## Sources and caveats
Government releases, Taiwanese media (PTS, CNA, UDN, Liberty Times, Commercial Times and
others) and Kollar, J. (2025) "Fallowing the fields, feeding the fabs", *EPE: Nature and
Space*, doi:10.1177/25148486251406484. Summaries are a finding aid: open the original
sources before citing. Records with inferred dates or unverified claims carry a note.

District boundaries: [taiwan-atlas](https://github.com/dkaoster/taiwan-atlas) (Ministry of the Interior data).
